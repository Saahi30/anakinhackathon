I'm building a hackathon project called *StockStrike*.

*The idea:* When a competitor goes out of stock on a platform like Myntra, Ajio, Blinkit, Zepto, or Amazon — there's a short window (hours) where their traffic is homeless. Customers click their listing, see OOS, and bounce back to search. Most brands miss this window entirely because nobody is watching competitor stock 24/7.

StockStrike monitors competitor product listings in real time, detects the moment they go out of stock, and alerts the brand's team immediately — so they can surge their ads, push their listings, and capture that traffic before anyone else does.

*The tool we must use:* anakin.io — it's an enterprise-grade web scraping API that can scrape any page with zero blocks, anti-detect browser, and returns clean markdown or structured JSON. We use it to scrape competitor product pages across multiple platforms and detect stock status changes.

*Context:* This is a hackathon build. We need a working demo. Keep things simple, functional, and impressive to show live to judges. We'll figure out architecture, stack, and implementation together in this terminal — just understand the idea deeply first and ask me what you need.
